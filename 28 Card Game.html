28 Card Game

How to play

Four players, two teams: you and Partner (top) against West and East. Each card is worth points: Jack 3, Nine 2, Ace 1, Ten 1, all others 0. Total 28.

Card strength: J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Highest bidder (14 to 28) secretly picks trump. Follow the led suit if you can. If you can’t, trump is revealed and trumps win the trick.

The bidding team must collect at least its bid in points to score +1; otherwise it scores −1.
